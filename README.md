# Avaron Company — Landing Page

Official landing page for Avaron Company, a product studio building AI-powered applications across hospitality, the creator economy, local discovery, and spiritual wellness.

---

## Products

### 01 · Resort Super Creator
A management platform built for resorts — not just booking, but a complete operational transformation using AI.

### 02 · Super Creator
A creator-brand matchmaking platform that removes fraudulent middlemen and connects creators with the right brand partnerships. Includes a full creator supply chain: resources, tools, deals, and community.

### 03 · Local Discovery
A city events and experiences app that lets you plan your month in advance, build a personal bucket list, and explore on the fly — everything in one place.

### 04 · Talk with God
A peace and wellness application featuring a divine AI voice inspired by Krishna from the Mahabharata. Users share their struggles and receive wisdom drawn from the Puranas and sacred texts across all faiths.
